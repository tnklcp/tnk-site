Our Services

Essential Care

  • Lawn Mowing: Regular mowing to maintain a neat, healthy lawn and proper grass height.
  • String Trimming: Precise trimming along sidewalks, driveways, and garden edges.
  • Blowing Off Hard Surfaces: Clearing leaves, clippings, and debris.
  • Proactive Property Inspection: Identify potential lawn or landscape issues early.

Standard Care

  • Includes Essential Care, Plus:
  • Pruning of Shrubs and Plants: Maintain healthy growth and shape.
  • Debris Clean-Up: Removal of fallen leaves, branches, and debris.

Premium Care

  • Includes Standard Care, Plus:
  • Hand Weeding of Garden Beds: Remove unwanted plants carefully.
  • Insulating Hose Spigots: Winter preparation to prevent frozen pipes.
  • Removing Cobwebs: Around entryways and structures.
  • Seasonal Fertilization: Promote healthy growth all year.

Storm Damage Protection Inspection

Comprehensive Property Assessment: We inspect trees, fences, gutters, and outdoor structures and recommend proactive measures to reduce storm damage year-round.
