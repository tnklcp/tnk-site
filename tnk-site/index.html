About Us

We grew up on the Oregon Coast, knocking on doors and offering to mow lawns, pull weeds—whatever we could do to help. Now, we're a full-service company delivering quality home care with that same hustle and heart.

Our Services

Essential Care

  • Mowing: Regular mowing to maintain a neat, healthy lawn and proper grass height.
  • Blowing Off Hard Surfaces: Clearing leaves, clippings, and debris.
  • Proactive Property Inspection: Identify potential lawn or landscape issues early.

Standard Care

  • Includes Essential Care, Plus:
  • Annual Garden Bed Weeding: Seasonal refresh of garden beds.
  • Annual Compost Refresh: Compost refresh for garden beds.
  • Pruning of Shrubs/Plants: As needed to maintain healthy growth.
  • Debris Clean-Up: Removal of leaves, branches, and debris.

Premium Care

  • Includes Standard Care, Plus:
  • Ongoing Garden Bed Weeding: As needed throughout the season.
  • Compost Refresh: Semi-annual compost refresh.
  • Gutter Cleaning: Semi-annual cleaning for proper drainage.
  • Hand Weeding: Garden beds and walkways.
  • Insulating Hose Spigots: As needed to prevent freeze damage.
  • Removing Cobwebs: Around entryways and structures.

Storm Damage Protection Inspection

Comprehensive Property Assessment: We inspect trees, fences, gutters, and outdoor structures and recommend proactive measures to reduce storm damage year-round.

Our Service Area

We proudly provide lawncare services along the Oregon Coast from Pacific City to Depoe Bay, including Neskowin, Otis, Rose Lodge, Lincoln City, and Gleneden Beach.
